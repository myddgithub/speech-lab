元音舌位图

右侧四边形按标准元音图：上高下低、前左后右。每个标准点左展右圆（i 左 y 右，ɤ 左 o 右，ɛ 左 ɔ 右）。低元音：前 a、央 A、后 ɑ。卷舌 ɚ 与央 ə 同高、略偏右。黄点是当前舌位。

剖面

单元音看舌位高低前后和唇形；复元音看滑移；鼻韵母看软腭下垂、气流转鼻腔。下巴会随开口度下降。点击阶段条可跳到该段。

口型与共振峰

剖面下方左侧是唇形前视图，右侧是当前音的共振峰示意图：上为频谱包络（F1 开口、F2 前后、F3），下为随动程变化的 F1–F3 轨迹。各音基频相同（F0 158 Hz）。

听音

韵母示意是嗓音合成，跟着动程走，用来听舌位和鼻音收尾，不是汉字读音。例词用系统语音读右侧例字。o 呼读是「喔」，与 uo「窝」分开。

四呼与省写

ong 归合口呼（与 ueng 配套），iong 归撮口呼。拼写省写：uei→ui，iou→iu，uen→un。不含 ê。

Vowel chart

The quadrilateral follows the conventional vowel chart: high at top, low at bottom, front at left, and back at right. At each standard point, left means spread and right means rounded (i–y, ɤ–o, ɛ–ɔ). Low vowels include front a, central A, and back ɑ; rhotic ɚ is near central ə. The yellow dot is the current tongue position.

Profile

Monophthongs show tongue height, frontness/backness, and lip shape; diphthongs and triphthongs show movement; nasal finals show the velum lowering and airflow entering the nose. The jaw follows mouth opening. Click a phase bar to jump to that part.

Lip shape and formants

The lower-left panel is a front view of the lips. The lower-right panel is a formant schematic: F1 reflects opening, F2 frontness/backness, and F3 provides an additional resonance cue. The lower trace shows F1–F3 along the movement path; all finals use the same teaching F0 of 158 Hz.

Listening

Final demo is voice synthesis following the tongue path and nasal ending, not a Chinese-character pronunciation. Example uses the system voice to read the example character when available. The hudu reading for o is “喔”, separate from uo “窝”.

Four mouth groups and spelling

ong belongs to the rounded-lip group (paired with ueng), while iong belongs to the pursed-lip group. Standard spelling abbreviations are uei→ui, iou→iu, and uen→un. The inventory does not include ê.
